Legal Meaning Checker

Paste a legal clause and its plain-language rewrite, in English or Hindi. The checker scores how well the rewrite keeps the clause's legal meaning on the 1–10 JUDGEBERT scale, decides whether it is preserved, and names the legal change it found.

About this checker

Not preserved · below 4.5A core rule changed: a duty, condition, number, deadline or party.
Partially preserved · 4.5 to 7.5A secondary detail is lost or blurred.
Preserved · 7.5 and aboveSame legal effect in plainer words.

Model

Method
Gradient-boosted trees (200 trees, depth 3) on 19 bilingual LegalDiff features, with a rule-based explainer
Training data
__NTRAIN__ EnHi-LMP pairs (80 clauses × English and Hindi × 1 faithful + 3 single-error rewrites), plus identical (→ 10) and unrelated (→ 1) augmentation
Labels
Rubric-based silver scores, 1–10

Measured accuracy

5-fold cross-validation grouped by clause (English and Hindi twins never split), mean of 3 seeds. FrJUDGE is the base paper's French dataset, scored by human annotators.

Known limits

  • Labels are silver; human double annotation is in progress.
  • Reads one sentence at a time, without definitions elsewhere in the contract.
  • Relies on word lists, so a meaning flip by a different verb ("does not cover" → "pays for") can be missed.
  • Faithful rewrites that add "you" or change wording a lot can score lower than they should.
  • A decision aid for reviewers, not legal advice.
Extends JUDGEBERT (Beauchemin et al., EMNLP 2025) to English, Hindi and tenancy law. Code and data: github.com/udbhav968-creator/NLP_RESERACH_WORK
